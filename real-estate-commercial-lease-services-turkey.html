Real Estate Law in Turkey — Key Facts

Property ownership and commercial leases are governed by different rules. The title record, property type, zoning position, parties and transaction structure must be assessed together.

Main property lawTurkish Civil Code No. 4721 and Land Registry Law No. 2644
Commercial leasesTurkish Code of Obligations No. 6098
Foreign individual limitUp to 30 hectares nationwide, subject to the statutory framework
District ownership limitUp to 10% of the privately owned area of the relevant district
Residence permitNot required merely to acquire real estate

Foreign natural persons may acquire qualifying real estate in Turkey subject to nationality eligibility, acreage limits, district-level ownership restrictions, security-zone rules and other Land Registry Law requirements.

A foreign natural person does not need a Turkish residence permit merely to acquire real estate, although identity, valuation and title-deed requirements still depend on the transaction.

The general statutory framework refers to up to 30 hectares nationwide for a foreign natural person, subject to the applicable rules and restrictions.

A company incorporated abroad has substantially narrower direct acquisition rights than a foreign individual and may require a specific statutory basis. A Turkish company with foreign shareholders may fall under a different Article 36 framework.
