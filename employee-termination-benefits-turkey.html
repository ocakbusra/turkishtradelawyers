Severance, Notice & Unemployment Pay Guide for Employees in Türkiye

June 2026 5 min read Updated: October 2, 2026

Severance, notice pay and unemployment benefit have separate eligibility tests. The SGK exit code reports the reason for leaving; it does not by itself establish or remove an employee’s legal entitlement. The termination facts, service history, contract and supporting records must be assessed.

The table below distinguishes common SGK codes and the questions to check. It assumes employment within the relevant labour-law framework; special employment regimes and disputed termination grounds require separate review.

Check Each Benefit Separately

  • Severance: Law No. 1475, Article 14 remains the core provision. Generally, at least one year of service and a qualifying termination route are needed; the statutory ceiling and the employee’s wage components affect calculation.
  • Notice: Labour Law No. 4857, Article 17 concerns indefinite-term employment and notice periods based on service. Notice pay arises when required notice is not provided or paid, subject to the termination route.
  • Unemployment benefit: İŞKUR assesses eligible involuntary or qualifying justified departures, coverage under an employment contract for the final 120 days, at least 600 days of unemployment premiums in the preceding three years, and an application within 30 days. Late application can reduce the payment period unless force majeure applies.

Documents to Review

Keep the SGK exit declaration, termination notice, employment contract, payroll and service records, correspondence supporting the termination ground, and any marriage, military-service, health or retirement evidence. Challenge an incorrect code or disputed reason through the appropriate process; do not treat the employer’s reporting entry as the final legal decision.
