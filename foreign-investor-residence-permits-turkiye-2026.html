Foreign Investor Residence Permits in Türkiye: Presidential Decision No. 11994

Immigration & Foreign Investment November 18, 2026 7 min read

In short: Presidential Decision No. 11994, published on 18 November 2026, establishes two investment thresholds for a residence permit under Article 31(1)(j) of Law No. 6458: at least USD 150,000 for three years or USD 200,000 for five years, or the equivalent in foreign currency. Only the investment routes listed in the Decision qualify.

Why it matters: Investors must select an eligible asset, obtain verification from the designated authority and maintain the investment for half the permit period. The Decision also covers specified foreign family members but expressly excludes these permits from the Article 12 citizenship route.

Decision No. 11994 at a Glance

The Decision was published in the Official Gazette of 18 November 2026, issue 33434, and entered into force on publication. It implements the investor category in Article 31(1)(j) and the duration provision in Article 31(5) of the Law on Foreigners and International Protection No. 6458.

Minimum qualifying investmentPermit durationRequired holding period
USD 150,000 or equivalent in foreign currency3 yearsHalf the permit period (18 months)
USD 200,000 or equivalent in foreign currency5 yearsHalf the permit period (30 months)

The 18- and 30-month figures are the arithmetic equivalents of the Decision's “half of the residence permit duration” requirement. The applicable threshold and timing should be assessed against the permit actually issued and the authority's verification.

Who Is Covered?

The route concerns a foreign national who does not work in Türkiye and makes a qualifying investment. Article 1 also covers that investor's foreign spouse and the minor or dependent foreign children of the investor and of the spouse. Family eligibility should be documented for each applicant; the Decision does not remove ordinary identity, relationship or residence formalities.

The investor route is a category of short-term residence permit. It should not be treated as a work authorisation or as a guarantee that every applicant meeting the investment threshold will receive a permit. The general short-term residence rules and the procedures to be set by the Ministry of the Interior still matter.

Which Investments Qualify?

Article 2 identifies four routes. For each one, a named public authority must verify that the required US-dollar amount or foreign-currency equivalent has been invested and that the relevant holding condition is met.

1. Deposit with a bank operating in Türkiye

The amount must be deposited with a bank operating in Türkiye and maintained for half the residence permit period. The Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BRSA/BDDK) verifies this route. Investors should confirm the account holder, currency, amount and restriction on withdrawal before relying on a deposit.

2. Government debt instruments

The investor may purchase government debt instruments and hold them for half the permit period. The Ministry of Treasury and Finance verifies this route. Records should show the instruments acquired, the investor's ownership and continued holding.

3. Real estate or venture capital investment fund units

Units in a real estate investment fund or a venture capital investment fund may qualify if held for half the permit period. The Capital Markets Board (CMB/SPK) verifies this route. A direct purchase of an apartment, commercial property or land is not one of the four routes listed in this Decision; fund units and direct property ownership are legally distinct.

4. Individual pension system contributions

The investor may contribute to the individual pension system by joining a pension plan within the scope determined by the Insurance and Private Pension Regulation and Supervision Agency (SEDDK). The investor must remain in the system for half the permit period, and SEDDK verifies the route. The plan's scope and contribution records should be checked before an application.

Verification and Application Planning

The investment amount alone does not complete the residence application. Article 2 requires verification by the authority responsible for the chosen asset. Article 3 leaves the services and other administrative procedures under the Decision to rules determined by the Ministry of the Interior. Applicants should therefore check the current filing route, documentary requirements and any implementing instructions before committing funds.

  1. Choose one of the four Article 2 investment routes and confirm the appropriate USD threshold or foreign-currency equivalent.
  2. Document the applicant's identity and beneficial ownership of the investment.
  3. Obtain the records needed for verification by BDDK, the Treasury and Finance Ministry, SPK or SEDDK, as applicable.
  4. Plan how the deposit, instrument, fund units or pension participation will remain in place for half the requested permit period.
  5. Prepare spouse and child relationship and dependency evidence where family members will apply.
  6. Check the current Migration Management guidance and e-Residence requirements for the application itself.

Before any withdrawal, sale or change of asset during the holding period, obtain case-specific advice on the consequences for the permit. The Decision states the holding condition but does not itself provide a complete procedural checklist.

What the Decision Does Not Grant

It does not create a citizenship entitlement. Article 4 says residence permits issued under this Decision are not evaluated under Article 12 of Turkish Citizenship Law No. 5901. Any citizenship analysis requires a separate legal basis and assessment.

It does not grant a work permit. The Decision's investor category is framed around a person who does not work in Türkiye. Investors who intend to take up employment or perform work should assess the separate work-authorisation rules.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Does USD 150,000 guarantee a three-year permit?

No. The amount is the threshold in the annexed list. The investment must fit an Article 2 route, be verified by the designated authority and satisfy the applicable residence procedures.

Can I use the equivalent of USD 150,000 or USD 200,000 in another currency?

Yes. The annex permits the equivalent in foreign currency. Applicants should obtain current guidance on valuation and the documentary evidence required by the verifying authority.

Does buying property directly qualify?

Not under the four investment routes in Article 2. The Decision lists units in a real estate investment fund, not direct property ownership.

How long do I need to keep the investment?

For half the duration of the residence permit. That is 18 months for a three-year permit and 30 months for a five-year permit.

Can my spouse and children apply?

Article 1 includes the foreign spouse and minor or dependent foreign children of the investor and spouse. Each person's documents and eligibility should be checked under the applicable procedures.

Can this permit be used for exceptional citizenship under Article 12?

No. Article 4 expressly excludes residence permits issued under this Decision from evaluation under Article 12 of Law No. 5901.
