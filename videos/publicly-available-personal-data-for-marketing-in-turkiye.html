Original video description

A landmark Turkish Constitutional Court ruling has reshaped how businesses can use publicly available personal data for marketing activities in Türkiye. The Court cancelled a significant administrative fine imposed on an insurance company that used a publicly listed phone number for marketing purposes. The decision confirms an important legal principle: administrative authorities cannot impose penalties based on guidance documents that go beyond the law itself. If your company conducts marketing, lead generation, sales outreach, debt collection, customer acquisition, or B2B prospecting in Türkiye, this decision may directly impact your compliance strategy and defense rights.
