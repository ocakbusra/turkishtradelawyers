Original video description

Are you planning to relocate? A new bill submitted to the Turkish parliament could change everything for remote workers, freelancers, and global investors. If passed, individuals who have not lived or paid taxes in Türkiye in the last three years could enjoy a 20 year tax exemption on all foreign income. You could live in Türkiye and legally pay zero Turkish income tax on your overseas earnings. While income generated locally within Türkiye is still taxed normally, this bill allows you to keep your global wealth completely tax free without even needing to file an annual return for it.
