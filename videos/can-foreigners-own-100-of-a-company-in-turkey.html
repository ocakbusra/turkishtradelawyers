How full foreign ownership works in Türkiye and where regulated sectors may impose separate approvals or licensing requirements.

Original video description

Foreign investors can generally own 100% of a company in Turkiye without having a Turkish shareholder. However, certain regulated sectors may have specific ownership, licensing or authorization requirements. This short video explains the basic rule for foreign entrepreneurs and companies considering doing business in Turkiye. General information on Turkish law.
