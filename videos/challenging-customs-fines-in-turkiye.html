Did your company recently pay a customs fine in Türkiye? You need to watch this before paying another one! On March 26, 2026, the Turkish Constitutional Court made a landmark decision regarding Article 241 of the Customs Law. The ruling changes everything: customs authorities can no longer impose administrative fines based solely on secondary regulations like circulars, communiqués, or administrative letters. The penalty must be defined in primary law! What importers and businesses in Türkiye should do right now: Check if your active customs fines were based only on secondary regulations. If you paid under protest, you may have grounds to litigate for a refund. Update your compliance checklists immediately.
