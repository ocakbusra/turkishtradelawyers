TURKISH LAW 101 - What Is a Minority Shareholder?

Corporate Law May 27, 2026 1:01

About this video

How company type and the articles of association affect who qualifies as a minority shareholder.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Original video description

Understanding minority shareholder status under the Turkish Commercial Code is essential for structuring joint ventures in the Turkish market. In private joint stock and limited liability companies, a minimum 10% capital share establishes minority status. For public joint stock companies, the statutory threshold is 5%. Company articles of association can lower these rates to grant further protection to smaller investors, but the law strictly prohibits increasing them.
