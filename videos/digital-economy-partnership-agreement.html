How a digital trade agreement between Türkiye and four other Turkic states addresses electronic transactions and data flows.

Original video description

The Digital Economy Partnership Agreement introduces a common legal framework for cross-border digital trade between Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic, Türkiye, and Uzbekistan. In this video, we explain the agreement’s practical implications for companies conducting online business across these five markets, including: • Cross-border payment transfers • Mutual recognition of electronic signatures • Digital contracts and electronic records • Paperless customs procedures • Cross-border electronic invoicing • Personal data protection and Türkiye’s KVKK • Digital marketing and anti-spam compliance Although the agreement facilitates regional digital trade, it does not remove local compliance obligations. Companies processing personal data, targeting consumers, using foreign service providers, or sending commercial electronic communications must review their privacy policies, data-transfer mechanisms, vendor contracts, and marketing practices. Understanding these requirements is essential for protecting cross-border operations and investments.
