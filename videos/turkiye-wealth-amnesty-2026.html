Türkiye Wealth Amnesty 2026: How the 0% Tax Rate Works

About this video

Which assets the 2026 wealth amnesty covers and when its zero percent tax rate may apply.

Original video description

Türkiye’s 2026 Wealth Amnesty provides a limited opportunity for individuals and companies to declare qualifying foreign or domestic assets and bring them into the Turkish financial system. Qualifying assets include money, gold, foreign currency, securities and other capital market instruments. The declaration period continues until 31 July 2027. The standard tax rate is 5%. However, reduced rates may apply where declared assets are held in specified Turkish financial instruments for the required commitment period. For qualifying declarations made by 31 December 2026, a commitment of at least five years may reduce the applicable tax rate to 0%. Foreign assets must generally be transferred to Türkiye within two months following the declaration. This video explains: • Who may benefit from the asset declaration regime • Which foreign and domestic assets qualify • The standard 5% tax rate • The conditions for reduced tax rates • How the 0% tax option works • The two-month transfer requirement • Tax examination and assessment protection • The consequences of breaching a holding commitment Tax protection applies only where all statutory conditions, payment obligations, recordkeeping rules and commitment periods are properly satisfied.
