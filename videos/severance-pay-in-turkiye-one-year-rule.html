Are you looking for a comprehensive guide on how severance pay works? Managing severance pay in Türkiye is one of the most critical aspects of employment compliance for international companies and foreign HR leaders. Under Turkish Labor Law, understanding the mechanics of severance pay is vital. Completing just one full year of employment triggers a strict legal obligation. This timeframe is significantly shorter than in many OECD countries, making accurate corporate budgeting essential. In this video, we break down: - The legal framework governing severance pay in Türkiye. - How the 1-year severance pay qualification threshold is calculated. - Critical differences in labor law compliance for foreign entities. - Why the actual employment start date determines your financial liabilities.
