Important Compliance Alert for the Hospitality Sector in Turkiye

Privacy and Compliance May 21, 2026 0:40

About this video

How hotels and other accommodation providers handle guest identification details and copies of identity documents.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Original video description

Hotels, motels, and short-term rental providers in Turkiye can no longer photocopy or scan guest ID cards or passports during check-in. In an effort to align with EU data protection standards, the KVKK rules that recording identity details is sufficient, and physical copies are now strictly prohibited. In this short video, we highlight what this means for your daily operations and why you must destroy previously collected copies to protect your business from major penalties.
