Enforcement of foreign judgments and local protective measures in commercial disputes between Spain and Türkiye.

Original video description

The Euro-Mediterranean corridor between Spain and Türkiye features a massive volume of trade, particularly for fashion retail brands and industrial components. Since Türkiye sits outside EU civil procedure regulations (like Brussels Recast), automatic recognition of Spanish court orders does not apply. However, local protective mechanisms offer rapid and robust solutions on the ground. In cases of manufacturing defaults or breach of distribution contracts, Spanish entities can launch rapid discovery actions, known as "Delil Tespiti", to legally record product defects before cargo is altered. To secure financial claims, companies can apply for a preliminary attachment (İhtiyati Haciz) to immediately freeze a default partner's local bank accounts and physical stock. Furthermore, under the 1928 Hague Convention, Spanish corporations are entirely exempt from posting financial security deposits (cautio judicatum solvi / teminat muafiyeti) to litigate in Turkish courts.
