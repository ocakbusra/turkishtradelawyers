Why are Hong Kong investors quietly moving millions of dollars into Türkiye right now? It all comes down to the new Hong Kong Türkiye BIT 2023. This Bilateral Investment Treaty acts as a massive legal and financial shield for foreign capital. It legally guarantees fair and equitable treatment, provides strict protection against expropriation, and most importantly, grants investors direct access to international arbitration if a dispute arises with the state. If you are expanding your operations from Asia to the Turkish market, relying on standard corporate structures is not enough. You need to leverage these international treaties to protect your wealth.
