AI Advertising Rules in Türkiye: Deepfakes, Disclosure and Fines

Disclosure requirements for AI-generated ads, digital characters, and targeted marketing in Türkiye.

Original video description

Türkiye’s amended Regulation on Commercial Advertising and Unfair Commercial Practices introduces new compliance requirements for AI-generated advertising, digital characters, targeted marketing and endorsements. Businesses must clearly disclose the use of artificial intelligence where it may materially influence consumer behaviour or where AI is used to create digital characters that cannot be distinguished from real people. Advertisements that use an AI-generated digital copy of a real person to falsely suggest that the person used, experienced or recommended a product are prohibited. This video explains what brands, e-commerce companies, marketing agencies and multinational businesses should review, including: • AI-generated advertising content • Deepfake and digital replica risks • Consumer disclosure requirements • Influencer and endorsement practices • Targeted advertising compliance • Advertising Board sanctions Companies operating or advertising in Türkiye should review their AI content processes and marketing contracts under the new rules.
