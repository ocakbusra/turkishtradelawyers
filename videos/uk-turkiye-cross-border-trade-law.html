How businesses can seek to protect assets and inventory in Türkiye while UK arbitration is underway.

Original video description

Even when UK-Türkiye supply contracts select English Law or London arbitration (via LCIA or ICC), local asset protection requires immediate, tactical local measures. English legal teams do not have to wait for months or years for a London arbitration tribunal to reach a final award while cross-border assets or inventories are at risk. Under Turkish procedural law, international buyers can concurrently apply to Turkish commercial courts for a preliminary attachment (İhtiyati Haciz) to legally lock down a supplier's warehouse inventories and local bank accounts. Furthermore, thanks to long-standing legal reciprocity rules between the UK and Türkiye, British corporations are fully exempt from posting heavy court security deposits (teminat muafiyeti), ensuring your operational capital remains free during litigation.
