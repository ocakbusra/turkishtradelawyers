What If a Debtor Objects to a Payment Order in Turkey?

Debt Collection Sep 29, 2026 0:51

About this video

How a debtor’s objection can affect enforcement and what options a creditor may have to challenge it.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Original video description

A debtor’s timely objection can suspend a standard debt enforcement proceeding in Türkiye. The creditor may then need to challenge the objection before the competent court. Additional compensation may be awarded if the relevant legal conditions are met.
