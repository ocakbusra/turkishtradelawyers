Tax and Trade Finance in Turkey — 2026 Snapshot

Rates and incentives depend on the transaction, taxpayer, activity, documents and current statutory framework. The following is a compact issue-spotting snapshot.

General corporate tax25% for 2026
Specified entities30% for specified financial-sector and PPP entities
VAT rates20% standard; 10% and 1% for listed supplies
Domestic minimum taxGenerally 10% before specified exclusions and deductions
Reverse-charge VATSpecified transactions may require VAT Return No. 2
Dividend withholding15% domestic rate, subject to treaty and exemptions
Investment incentivesPresidential Decree No. 9903 and Communiqué No. 2025/1
Payment securityUCP 600, URDG 758 and other instruments may apply
