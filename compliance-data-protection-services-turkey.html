KVKK Compliance in Turkey — Key Facts

Turkish data protection law is built around Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data — KVKK. The Personal Data Protection Authority and Board enforce the framework through registry obligations, information and security duties, transfer rules, decisions and administrative fines.

2026 KVKK Snapshot

2024 Article 6 and 9 reformSpecial-category processing and cross-border transfer rules materially changed.
Cross-border transfersUse adequacy decisions, appropriate safeguards or limited incidental-transfer circumstances where statutory conditions are met.
Turkish standard contractsFour Board templates: Controller → Controller, Controller → Processor, Processor → Processor and Processor → Controller.
Five-business-day notificationExecuted standard contracts must be notified to the Authority within five business days.
VERBİS ordinary local exemptionGenerally below 50 employees and below TRY 100 million annual balance-sheet total, where the other conditions are satisfied.
Special-category exemptionUnder Decision No. 2025/1572, qualifying controllers may be exempt below 10 employees and below TRY 10 million balance sheet.
Notice and consentDecision No. 2026/347 requires separate structuring where processing relies on explicit consent.
Biometric attendanceDecision No. 2026/921 materially changes the risk analysis for biometric data used solely for employee attendance.

Last legal review: September 2026. Thresholds, Board decisions and fine ranges should be reviewed whenever the Authority issues an update.

4. VERBİS Registration & Foreign Data Controller Representation

VERBİS is the Turkish Data Controllers’ Registry Information System. Registration is the public-facing output of a wider data-mapping exercise, not an isolated portal form. We advise on whether registration is required, applicable exemptions, employee and balance-sheet thresholds, principal activity, special-category processing, foreign-controller status, representative structure, registry categories, updates and compliance evidence.

Local controllers

For an ordinary local controller whose principal activity is not special-category processing, the general exemption generally requires both fewer than 50 annual employees and less than TRY 100 million annual balance-sheet total.

Special-category activity

Decision No. 2025/1572 introduced a separate qualifying exemption where employees are fewer than 10 and the annual balance-sheet total is below TRY 10 million.

Foreign controllers

Non-resident controllers should be assessed separately. Local employee and balance-sheet exemptions should not be assumed to apply automatically.

Representation

Where the applicable framework requires a Turkish compliance contact, we advise on appointment, mandate, VERBİS, Authority communications and data-subject correspondence.

5. Privacy Notices, Transparency & Explicit Consent

We draft and review KVKK privacy notices and clarification texts for websites, e-commerce, mobile applications, customers, prospects, employees, candidates, CCTV, visitors, suppliers, business contacts, call centres, loyalty programmes, events and campaigns. A compliant notice should accurately address controller identity, processing purposes, recipients or transfers, collection method, legal basis and data-subject rights.

Decision No. 2026/347: notice and consent are separate

Where consent is required, the privacy notice should not be treated as something the individual “consents to”. The privacy information / clarification text and explicit-consent text should be structured separately.

Explicit consent is one lawful processing condition, not a catch-all. We test whether consent is actually required, whether it is specific and freely given, whether purposes are bundled, whether withdrawal works and whether the record can be proved.

We also review employee, health, biometric, loyalty, profiling, marketing, cross-border and mobile-app consent flows. Review privacy notices or consent flows.

6. Special Category Data, Employee Privacy & Biometrics

KVKK Article 6 regulates special categories such as health, biometric, genetic, criminal conviction, religion, philosophical belief, political opinion, association / foundation / union membership, appearance or clothing-related protected information and sexual life. We advise on the correct Article 6 condition, employment processing, access controls, encryption, transfer, retention and additional technical or administrative safeguards.

Employee privacy work covers recruitment, HR, payroll, bank, performance, disciplinary, CCTV, access control, health, disability, background checks, monitoring, email and device use, remote work, global HR systems, payroll vendors, cross-border transfers and retention after termination. Employment imbalance means consent should not be assumed to be the safest basis merely because a signature can be obtained.

Biometric attendance after Decision No. 2026/921

The decision concerns biometric data used solely for employee attendance tracking and points businesses toward less intrusive alternatives such as cards, PINs, RFID / NFC access cards or conventional signatures. Other biometric uses must still be assessed separately by purpose, necessity, legal basis and proportionality.

7. Cross-Border Personal Data Transfers From Turkey

Turkish data may need to move to global or regional headquarters, cloud infrastructure, SaaS providers, CRM vendors, payroll and HR platforms, security providers, analytics tools, customer-support systems, group companies or external advisers. The 2024 Article 9 reform introduced a staged analysis:

  1. Check adequacy: assess whether the destination, sector or international organisation benefits from an applicable adequacy decision.
  2. Identify an appropriate safeguard: consider Turkish standard contracts, binding corporate rules, qualifying public-sector arrangements or written undertakings approved by the Board.
  3. Use incidental grounds carefully: limited exceptions should not become a routine operating model.

We map the exporting entity, recipient role, destination, domestic legal basis, Article 9 mechanism, onward transfers, processor chain, cloud architecture, special-category data, safeguards, evidence and regulator notification.

8. Turkish Standard Contracts & Binding Corporate Rules

For many international businesses, the Turkish standard contract is the most practical Article 9 safeguard. The Board publishes four templates: Controller → Controller, Controller → Processor, Processor → Processor and Processor → Controller. Selection depends on the actual role of each party, not the label used in a global template.

We assist with role analysis, template selection, annex completion, data categories, data subjects, purposes, recipient groups, technical safeguards, special-category safeguards, onward transfers and the five-business-day notification.

July 2026 execution practice

The Authority announcement highlights signatures by authorised persons, signature on the Turkish text, supporting authority documents and authentication or notarised Turkish translation requirements for foreign documents. Amendment limits and the notification file should be checked before signature.

For group transfers, we assess whether binding corporate rules are appropriate, prepare the application structure and align the internal framework with the actual entities, transfers and data-subject rights.

11. Data Breach Response, Board Investigations & Defence

When a breach occurs, the legal response must run alongside containment and forensic work. We help structure the first response, identify affected data and special categories, assess the controller and processor roles, coordinate the notification record, assess data-subject communication and preserve evidence.

The 72-hour point must be described accurately

KVKK Article 12 requires notification “as soon as possible”. Board Decision No. 2019/10 interprets notification to the Board as no later than 72 hours after awareness. The 72-hour formulation should not be presented as the literal wording of Article 12.

For a Board investigation or information request, we align the response with the inventory, notices, VERBİS, contracts, actual processing, security measures and remediation plan. We can support authority correspondence, data-subject communications, internal governance and the defence record.

Frequently Asked Questions About KVKK and Data Protection in Turkey

What is KVKK?

KVKK is Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, the principal Turkish framework governing personal-data processing, data-controller duties, data-subject rights, registry obligations, transfers and security.

Is KVKK the same as GDPR?

No. The frameworks share concepts, but Turkish legal bases, notices, consent architecture, VERBİS, Article 9 transfers and local regulator practice require a Turkey-specific analysis.

Does every Turkish company need to register with VERBİS?

No. Exemptions and thresholds apply in some cases, and foreign-controller status must be assessed separately. The ordinary local exemption generally requires both the employee and balance-sheet conditions to be satisfied.

Can explicit consent be used for every processing activity?

No. The correct Article 5 or Article 6 condition should be identified for each purpose. Unnecessary consent can obscure the real legal basis and create operational risk.

Can we modify the Board’s Turkish standard contract?

The Board’s current practice and guidance should be checked before signature. The parties should preserve the required structure, complete the annexes accurately and assess any proposed amendment with care.

Do strictly necessary cookies require explicit consent?

Not automatically. The actual purpose and applicable statutory processing condition must be assessed. Non-essential cookies should rely on valid prior opt-in consent where no other lawful basis applies.

How long do we have to answer a data-subject request?

The request should be handled within the statutory response framework, with identity, scope, applicable exemptions and evidence tracked. We can design the intake and response workflow.

Can Turkish Trade Lawyers respond to a KVKK Authority letter?

Yes. We can review the request, map it to the actual processing and evidence, coordinate the response and support the remediation record.

Can you review our website for KVKK compliance?

Yes. We can review notices, consent flows, cookies, forms, analytics, marketing, vendors, transfers, retention and the connection between the public-facing materials and the internal inventory.

Can you perform a GDPR-to-KVKK gap analysis?

Yes. We compare the global programme against Turkish requirements and deliver a keep / localise / rebuild matrix with prioritised remediation.
