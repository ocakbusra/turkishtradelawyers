Foreign Companies Bidding in Türkiye: EKAP Registration and Tender Guide

Public Procurement & Market Entry September 28, 2026 9 min read

In short: Foreign legal persons can use the Public Procurement Authority’s EKAP registration route, but account registration does not guarantee eligibility for a particular tender. The Authority says foreign documents should be equivalent to the requested documents under the bidder’s home law. Check the live EKAP foreign-person registration guidance, current platform workflow and tender documents before bidding.

Who should read this: This guide is for foreign suppliers, contractors, technology firms and potential consortium partners.

What EKAP Is and Why Foreign Bidders Need to Plan Early

EKAP is Türkiye’s Electronic Public Procurement Platform, operated by the Public Procurement Authority. A foreign company may need a correctly registered legal-person account and authorised users before it can complete platform actions for a procurement. The Authority has published guidance on registration of foreign natural and legal persons, but the procedure, evidence and tender rules must be checked in their current form for each bid.

Registration is an administrative platform step. It does not guarantee that a bidder may participate in every procurement, satisfy a qualification condition or win a contract. Eligibility depends on the procurement law, tender notice and documents, the procurement method, any lawful participation conditions and the bidder’s evidence.

Foreign Company Registration: Evidence and Authority

The Public Procurement Authority’s published registration notice states that foreign persons may register on EKAP and that documents issued under the law of the country where the person is established should be equivalent to the documents requested. In practice, prepare current corporate registry evidence, proof of legal existence, representative and signing-authority documents, address and contact information, and any identifier requested by the platform. Check whether documents require apostille, consular certification, notarised Turkish translation or another formal step.

Confirm the current EKAP route for the legal form and country of the bidder. Foreign identity or potential tax identification details may be relevant to the account process, but users should follow the live platform instructions and current Public Procurement Authority guidance rather than rely on an older procedural summary. Give the registration process enough lead time for missing or inconsistent documents to be corrected.

Read the Tender Before Building the Bid

Start with the tender documents, not a generic checklist. Identify eligibility conditions, technical specifications, experience requirements, financial criteria, permitted consortium or subcontractor arrangements, language rules, bid-security requirements, contract terms and the electronic submission deadline. Where the notice describes local participation or domestic-goods preferences, assess how those provisions affect the foreign bidder and the goods or services offered.

Foreign bidders should map each requested certificate or record to its issuing authority and a Turkish equivalent. A company extract, tax standing certificate, audited accounts, professional qualification or work-experience record may not have a direct one-to-one counterpart. Prepare a translation and evidence note explaining the legal nature of the document without changing its substance.

Bid Preparation and Platform Controls

  1. Confirm participation conditions. Review the applicable procurement law and tender-specific rules for foreign bidders, joint ventures, local-content preferences and any sector-specific limitation.
  2. Complete registration early. Verify the bidder account, authorised user, electronic signature, contact email and any tax or foreign-entity identifier required by the current EKAP workflow.
  3. Build a document matrix. For every tender requirement, list the evidence, issuing body, validity date, translation, certification and responsible owner.
  4. Check the commercial submission. Review pricing currency and format, taxes, delivery obligations, contract security, price adjustment and payment terms as set out in the documents.
  5. Test submission readiness. Confirm file format and size limits, signature authority, platform access and the exact close time. Do not leave electronic submission to the final minutes.
  6. Retain the record. Keep the tender notice, questions and answers, submitted files, platform receipts, approvals and communication with consortium members.

Contracting and Delivery Risks

Winning a tender can create obligations beyond the initial bid price. Review performance security, delivery timetable, acceptance testing, change control, intellectual property, data processing, subcontracting, delay remedies, termination, dispute resolution and governing law. If the foreign bidder plans to perform through a Turkish subsidiary, branch, distributor or subcontractor, verify that the chosen model is allowed by the tender and contract.

Coordinate procurement, finance, tax, technical and local legal teams. The bidder should understand who signs the bid, who can bind the company, how a parent guarantee is issued, and whether any local registration or post-award licence is required. A platform account does not cure a missing authorisation or qualification in the tender file.

Use Current Official Instructions

The Public Procurement Authority’s foreign-registration page was published in 2019 and remains a useful starting reference, not a substitute for live platform instructions. Before a tender, verify current EKAP rules, notices, user guides, electronic-signature requirements and any amendments to Law No. 4734 or secondary legislation. Keep a dated copy of the procurement documents used for the decision.

Foreign Bidder Readiness Checklist

  1. Read the tender notice and all procurement documents for foreign-participation conditions and qualification requirements.
  2. Begin EKAP registration early and verify account identity, user authority, electronic signature and required identifiers.
  3. Create a matrix of each corporate, financial, technical and experience document and its Turkish equivalent.
  4. Confirm translation, apostille/consular certification, validity period and tender-language requirements.
  5. Review bid security, performance security, tax, delivery and post-award obligations before pricing.
  6. Test upload and signing steps and preserve the submitted documents and platform receipt.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a foreign company register on EKAP?

The Public Procurement Authority has published a registration route for foreign natural and legal persons. The foreign entity must complete the current registration and evidence requirements; registration alone does not establish eligibility for a specific tender.

Can every foreign company bid for every Turkish public contract?

No. Participation depends on the applicable procurement law, the tender documents, the procurement method, any permitted participation conditions and the bidder’s qualifications. Check the specific notice and current rules before preparing a bid.

How are foreign corporate documents assessed?

The Public Procurement Authority’s registration guidance states that documents issued under the foreign person’s home-country law should be equivalent to the documents requested. The exact evidence, translation, certification and authentication requirements should be confirmed against the current EKAP process and tender.

Does EKAP registration replace a tax number or electronic signature?

No. Registration, tax identification, electronic-signature, account-authority and tender-specific document requirements are distinct. Confirm which identifiers and credentials apply to the foreign entity and its authorised user.

What should a foreign bidder do first?

Review the tender notice and procurement documents, verify foreign participation conditions, map qualification and experience evidence, complete EKAP registration early, and confirm bid security, signing authority, translation and submission deadlines.
